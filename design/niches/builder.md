# Builder look: Blueprint

- Feels like plans on a site table: thin ruler lines, numbered items (01, 02, 03), square corners.
- Orange only on buttons and the small numbers. Never orange backgrounds.
- Headlines are condensed, heavy and uppercase; body copy stays plain and readable.
- Story demo to pair with: templates/stories/builder.html (blueprint becomes a house as you scroll).
- Words: name the suburbs and the real jobs (extensions, decks, bathrooms, knock-down rebuilds).
