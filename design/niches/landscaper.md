# Landscaper look: Garden

- Soft, organic: rounded cards, pill buttons, lots of breathing room.
- Green only for buttons and small accents; the page stays stone and ink.
- Serif headings in sentence case. No leaf emoji, no clip-art trees.
- Story demo to pair with: templates/stories/landscaper.html (messy yard to pristine garden).
- Words: before-and-after language, the suburbs they cover, the real jobs (turf, retaining walls, hedges, clean-ups).
