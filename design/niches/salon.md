# Salon look: Atelier

- Feels like a printed boutique menu: fine serif, thin lines, centred hero, generous space.
- Plum-rose only on buttons and fine rules. No pink gradients, no sparkles.
- Services read like a menu list with hairline separators.
- Story demo to pair with: templates/stories/salon.html (cut, curls and colour as you scroll).
- Words: warm and personal, first names, the suburb, booking made easy.
