OPTIONAL

Card payments and autopay

Bank transfer is free and stays the main way to pay. If you want a card option, make a free Stripe account, create a Payment Link there and paste it here; it is printed on your invoices. Stripe takes about 1.7% + 30c per Australian card payment and has no monthly fee. For monthly plans, a Stripe subscription link with BECS Direct Debit costs about 1% + 30c, capped at A$3.50. AgencyOS never holds a Stripe key.
