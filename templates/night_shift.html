Every night after {{ start_hour }} am the agents work your search areas on their own: find businesses, check their websites, match a story mock-up and draft the message. In the morning it all waits on Home under "Ready for you". Nothing is ever sent or published without your tap.

SWITCH

{% if settings.enabled %}The night shift is on{% else %}The night shift is off{% endif %}

{% if paused %}The agency is paused, so no shift will run until you press Start on Home. {% endif %}It runs inside AgencyOS, so the launcher must be open on your Mac. If the Mac was asleep at 2 am, the shift runs as soon as AgencyOS is open again that day.

Make the Mac wake itself at 2 am (one-time, optional)

Open the file deploy/night-shift/Wake Mac for Night Shift.command in the AgencyOS folder. It asks for your Mac password once, then the Mac wakes at 2:02 am every night when it is plugged in. To undo it, open Stop Mac Waking.command in the same folder.
